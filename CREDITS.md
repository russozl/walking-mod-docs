# Credits and References

## Camera Shake Reference

**Camera Shakify** (Blender Add-on)
https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/camera-shakify/

Used as visual reference for realistic handheld camera motion. The Walking Mod's camera motion system was inspired by the multi-frequency noise layering approach used in Camera Shakify.

Camera Shakify is a paid Blender add-on by Ian Hubert. The Walking Mod does not use any of its code - only the concept of layered frequency noise for realistic camera shake.

## Techniques

The Phone Camera system uses Second-Order Dynamics (spring-damper physics), a technique documented by t-machine.org and used in AAA games like The Last of Us and Red Dead Redemption 2.

The Intention Filter is based on Electronic Image Stabilization (EIS) principles used in phone cameras - detecting intentional movement direction and filtering perpendicular hand tremor.
